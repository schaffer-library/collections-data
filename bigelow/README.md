# John Bigelow Papers (Bigelow Collection)

## Overview
The **John Bigelow Papers** consist of the extensive correspondence of Bigelow and his family, his scrapbooks and writings, records and correspondence detailing his professional activities, diaries and journals belonging to Bigelow and other family members, genealogical documents and records of the Bigelow family, and a variety of photographs.

Funding for the digitization of this collection was provided by the **National Historical Publications & Records Commission (NHPRC)**.
