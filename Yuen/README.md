Shui Kong Yuen Photographs (Special Collections & Archives)
================================================================


In Special Collections and Archives
-----------------------------------

Overview
--------
The collection is primarily photographs taken by Shui Kong Yuen (1917-1992) of Union College campus and campus life. Topics of the photographs include student life, faculty and staff photos, photos of buildings and scenes on campus, fraternity life, concerts, commencement, personal photographs of Yuen and his family, and more. The photos take up a bulk of the collection, with a few scrapbooks as well. Extra value is placed on the captions and titles of his pieces, which give added context to the collection. Yuen was an international student from China, who attended Union College in the 1930s and 1940s, graduated in 1942. The collection shows not only Union life but also life pre-war and during the war. Most of the pictures are from 1940-1945, but the entire collection spans from 1938 to 1984.
