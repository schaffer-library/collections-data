Overview
--------
The collection consists primarily of writings by **Charles Proteus Steinmetz** for General Electric and for publication, as well as research notes and ledgers. Topics include research in thermodynamics and steam turbines, fraternities, and national issues such as Socialism. The collection also includes several books owned by his father Karl, as well as a photograph and some assorted ephemera. Charles Proteus Steinmetz (b. 1865–d. 1923) was a mathematician and electrical engineer. Forced to leave Germany because of Socialist activities in 1888, he became a professor at Union College and chair of the Department of Engineering.
