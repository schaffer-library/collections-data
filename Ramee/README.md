Overview
--------
Joseph Ramée was a European architect born in France, but he often traveled around Europe and the US and adopted styles from the places he visited. In January 1813, Eliphalet Nott came into contact with Ramée and found that their ideas for higher education aligned. Nott chose Ramée as the architect for Union College. Ramées designs included the Nott Memorial building as well as Schaffer Library. His style uses simple shapes as well as inspiration from his time and travels in Europe, such as Belgium, Germany, and Denmark.
