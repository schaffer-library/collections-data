Overview
--------
William Stanley Jr. (1858-1916) was an inventor and engineer who held 129 patents. He was born in Brooklyn NY and studied at Yale. He made developments to the field of electricity including the first practical transformer which spurred the development of AC power. His business, Stanley Manufacturing Company in Pittsfield, Massachusetts, was eventually bought by General Electric. The collection includes consists of the papers of William Stanley Jr. (1858-1916) and records of the Stanley Electric Manufacturing Company and its successor, General Electric Pittsfield Works. The collection consists of research notes, laboratory notebooks, sketches, scrapbooks, publications, photographs, and correspondence. He passed in 1916 of tuberculosis.
