Sheldon Jackson Collection (Special Collections & Archives)
==========================================================

Overview
--------
The Sheldon Jackson Collection documents the life and work of **Sheldon Jackson (1834–1909)**—a Presbyterian minister, missionary, and political leader—through correspondence, organizational records, and materials that evidence his role in establishing Presbyterian communities and government agencies across Alaska and the American West. The collection includes documentation of the **First Presbyterian Church of Sitka**, the **Museum of Alaskan Natural History and Ethnology**, and extensive family correspondence illuminating Jackson’s personal and professional networks.
